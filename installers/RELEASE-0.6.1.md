# Finanzblick 0.6.1

## Highlights

- Language selection is available before unlocking or creating a financial profile.
- Wealth and spending allocations use compact split layouts, coordinated donut segments and expandable grouped categories.
- Account, category and transaction views are denser, clearer and consistently localized.
- The sidebar prioritizes overview, wealth and transactions while keeping management areas visually grouped.
- Balance-backed import deduplication removes extraction duplicates while retaining genuine repeated payments.
- The synthetic demo includes an employee-share account with annual Apple share grants.
